# Sneha’s 21st Birthday Website

## What I’ll build
- A warm pink-to-red birthday card experience with subtle hearts, sparkles, parchment texture, and floating candle details.
- An animated opening screen with “Happy 21st Birthday, Sneha!”, a playful subtitle, and an envelope-style “Open Your Card” interaction.
- A live friendship counter calculated from November 23, 2024 in days, hours, and minutes.
- An eight-photo placeholder gallery with enchanted tilt/glow interactions and a click-to-enlarge viewer.
- Eight editable envelope notes that open to reveal placeholder messages.
- A “Make a Wish” heart-confetti burst, an interactive candle to extinguish and relight, and a playful birthday-title generator.
- A personal closing message and editable signature placeholder.

## Design direction
- Blush pink, dark red, warm cream, and restrained gold accents.gradient
- Rounded friendly headings, readable body text, soft paper surfaces, minimal heart motifs.
- Gentle motion throughout, with reduced-motion support and touch-friendly behavior on phones.

## Technical details
- Keep photos and message text in simple arrays near the top of the page file for easy replacement.
- Use responsive layouts, semantic sections, keyboard-accessible dialogs and cards, and smooth scrolling.
- Add page-specific title and social metadata.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including the counter, gallery viewer, notes, candle, and wish interaction.
